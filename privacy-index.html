Privacy Policy — RozKhoj - Jobs In Pakistan

Effective date: 24 November 2026

RozKhoj - Jobs In Pakistan ("the app") is developed by F-Tech Developers. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

The app does not require any account or registration, and it does not ask for your name, phone number, email address or any personal identity information.

  • Job content: The app downloads job posts from our own website, rozkhoj.pk, and stores a copy on your device so the app works offline. This content contains no personal information about you.
  • Saved jobs: Jobs you save are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us.
  • Tool inputs: Information you type into the app's tools (such as Age Calculator, CV Maker, Salary Calculator, Merit Calculator and Typing Test) is processed on your device only and is not transmitted to us or stored by us.
  • Notifications: The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send job alerts. Firebase generates a device token used only to deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Advertising: The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use the device advertising ID and related technical data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. How information is used

  • To display job posts, categories and alerts in the app.
  • To send notifications about new jobs and closing dates (you can turn notifications off any time in your phone's settings).
  • To show advertisements that keep the app free.

3. Third-party services

The app uses these Google services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging and Cloud Firestore (notifications and job-field data)

Job posts are loaded from rozkhoj.pk. Links inside posts and advertisements may open third-party websites; we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade or share your personal information with anyone, because the app does not collect personal information from you. Technical data handled by Google services is governed by Google's policies as described above.

5. Children

The app is intended for job seekers and is not directed at children under the age of 13.

6. Security

Data stored by the app (saved jobs and cached posts) stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
